Community Guidelines

  • No bullying, harassment, or hate speech.
  • Respect everyone's feelings, opinions, and experiences.
  • Do not share anyone's private information.
  • No threats, discrimination, or harmful messages.
  • Keep the Freedom Wall safe and welcoming for everyone.
  • Think before posting and report inappropriate content.

Posting here is anonymous by default, but anonymous does not mean untraceable — please post responsibly. Messages that break these guidelines may be removed.
