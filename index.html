Wall guidelines

  • Be kind. No bullying, hate, or harassment.
  • Don’t name or point out specific students or teachers.
  • Keep personal details off the wall: phone numbers, addresses, and accounts.
  • No sexual, graphic, or violent content.
  • Notes are public, so only post what you’re comfortable having others read.

If you’re struggling, talk to a trusted adult or your school’s guidance office. Report notes that break these rules. Notes with several reports are hidden.
